THE FOLD PHILOSOPHY

Get dressed.
Get out there.

WIZZY FOLD is a fictional independent clothing label built around one simple idea: the best outfit is the one that feels like you. We take familiar everyday silhouettes, give them a considered point of view, and leave the rest to the person wearing them.

Our visual language is bold but uncomplicated. Our pieces are designed to live together, be worn often, and collect a few stories along the way.
